## 3. Mandarin–English Translation Quality Evaluation

**Task:** Evaluate the quality of an AI-generated translation.

**Source Text:**

"We need to break the ice before starting the negotiation."

**AI-Generated Translation:**

"我们需要打破冰块再开始谈判。"

### Evaluation

The translation is understandable at a literal level but fails to convey the intended idiomatic meaning of "break the ice".

The expression refers to creating a comfortable atmosphere and establishing rapport before beginning a discussion or negotiation.

**Recommended Translation:**

"我们需要先建立融洽的沟通氛围，再开始谈判。"

### Evaluation Findings

| Category | Original AI Translation | Revised Translation |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning accuracy | 3/5 | 5/5 |
| Natural Mandarin expression | 2/5 | 5/5 |
| Cultural and contextual suitability | Needs improvement | 5/5 |
| Business suitability | Needs improvement | 5/5 |

**Feedback:**

The revised translation better reflects the intended meaning and is more appropriate for professional Chinese business communication.

## 7. Speech Recognition Error Detection Example

**Incorrect ASR Output:**

我们下午三点开会讨论项目近展。

**Correct Transcription:**

我们下午三点开会讨论项目进展。

### Error Analysis

- **Error Type:** Character recognition error
- **Issue:** "近展" is incorrect in this business context.
- **Correction:** "进展"
- **Reason:** "项目进展" is the standard Mandarin business expression meaning project progress.

**Evaluation Feedback:**

The incorrect character changes the accuracy of the intended expression. Human review identifies and corrects the recognition error while preserving the original meaning.
